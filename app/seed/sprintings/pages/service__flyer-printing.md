#### Flyer Specs
- Accepted File Formats: PDF, DOC, JPEG, JPG, PNG, TIFF, AI, PSD, PUB, PPT.
- Resolution: 300 DPI.
- Color Space: We prefer CMYK (Cyan/Magenta/Yellow/Black). RGB colors may turn out slightly differently.
- Fonts: Please flatten your documents before sending your file. Specialized fonts may not be recognized and will be replaced with another in our system.
- Paper: Glossy or matte, recycled or uncoated, thick or premium, we have the best paper stock with options that you will love.
