## Folded Business Cards
Folded business cards give you more room for customization so they can make twice the impression of a regular card. Use the extra space to add additional images, information, and selling points, or use them for practical purposes such as important dates, schedules, or reminders.
Folded business cards are the perfect solution when you need something that is above and beyond the standard business card. They allow you to stand out from the crowd so you can draw extra attention to your company's products and services. Many of our clients choose to order these as tent business cards to place in their retail stores, galleries, restaurants, salons, and other venues.
Maximize your marketing efforts with custom folded business cards. You'll love how versatile they are, whether you use them as appointment cards, loyalty cards, mini menus, mini calendar cards, event and sales promotions, and much more. With our extensive variety of paper stocks to choose from, you'll have no trouble finding a folded card that fits your needs.

## More Info:
Sleek, chic, and definitely not meek, folded business cards are the quintessential tool for the enthusiastic networker.
- Designing an appointment card or want to write on the inside of your card? Choose the 14 pt. Uncoated Cover or select High Gloss UV Coating Front Only on the 14 pt. Gloss Cover.
- For the 14 pt. Gloss paper stock, a High Gloss UV Coating will be applied to the color sides, unless High Gloss UV Coating Front Only is selected. High Gloss UV Coating will not be applied to the back if you select Grayscale Back or Blank Back. High Gloss UV Coating makes the products more durable, so your postcards will last longer. You cannot write or print on products with High Gloss UV Coating.
- Our High Gloss UV Coating Front Only option may produce variation in color and brightness between the front and back of your product.
- Want to take your prints to the next level? Choose 16 pt. Matte Cover paper stock and take advantage of our Raised UV or Raised Foil specialty finishes.
- Please note that due to the fact that we print in large volumes, different paper types may have different quantity options

**Q: Can I upload my own folded business card design?**
Of course - please start by downloading the correct template for the size you wish to order. All of our templates are conveniently marked with the safe zone, trim, and bleed lines. The safe zone is the area where all your critical elements (text, images, logos) should be placed so that they are not cut off during the trimming process. The bleed is the part outside of the safe zone that gets trimmed off when the product is cut to its final size. We require a 0.1" bleed for folded business cards.
Once your file is ready, simply upload your design and place your order. We have 2 proof options - see an instant online proof (free) or request a manually processed PDF proof to make sure your file has been set up correctly for print.
**Q: I need help with my design. Do you offer any pre-made templates?**
Designing a folded business card is easy with our free Online Designer tool – no graphic designer or expensive software needed! Customize any of our modern, professionally-designed templates with your company name and text. You can even upload your own logo, photos, and other graphics. Once you are happy with your personalized design, place your order and we'll send it straight to print.
**Q: How long will it take to get my folded business cards?**
The production time for most folded cards is 3 to 5 business days. (The day after you have approved your proof and your payment has been processed is considered the first day of production.) If you need a quicker turnaround, rush printing is available for many of our products. Please allow an additional 2 to 6 business days for the cards to arrive at your doorstep; you will have the option to select expedited shipping at checkout if needed.
