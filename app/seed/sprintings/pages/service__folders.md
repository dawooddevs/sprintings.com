## Folders
Custom folders keep relevant material organized and are perfect for handing out at business meetings and sales pitches.
- This product is sent assembled.
- When submitting files, make sure all text and graphics for the pockets are upside down. Folders are printed flat and the pockets will fold up into the inside of the finished product.
- Make sure all text is legible. Anything under 10 pt. will be hard to read.
- Choose from a wide selection of sizes pocket/business card slit options. Our business card slits are suitable for standard 2" x 3.5" business cards.
- Choose our Gloss stock for a beautiful shine or the 14 pt. Uncoated Cover for a textured look to your folder.
- For the 14 pt. Gloss Cover, a High Gloss UV Coating will be applied to the Front (outside) only. High Gloss UV Coating makes the products more durable, so your folders will last longer. You cannot write or print on products with High Gloss UV Coating.
