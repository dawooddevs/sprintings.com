## Club Flyers
Get the word out about your club, venue, or upcoming event with high-quality club flyers from GotPrint.com. Whether you have your own design or use one of our free online templates, our quick turnaround and affordable prices mean you can have your club flyers in a matter of days.
We have a variety of options, so you can order a custom club flyer that fits your needs and your budget. With our state-of-the-art printing equipment, we can deliver stunning club flyers that reflect well upon you and your brand. We are here to help you make a stellar first impression!
When ordering club flyers, select from a wide range of sizes and paper stocks to ensure the final product looks just right. Choose between vertical or horizontal orientations or a unique square (4.75" x 4.75") shape. Whether you need general promotional flyers, party flyers, concert flyers, or other types of event flyers, you can make them stand out even more by adding extra touches such as rounded corners or printing them on ultra-thick luxury paper. Whether you prefer a glossy finish to make your images pop or a matte finish for a more subtle approach, we can help you create a handout that makes it clear you're the hottest venue in town! Ordering club flyers from GotPrint is quick, easy and budget-friendly. Simply upload your file or choose one of our professionally-designed templates to get started!

## More Info:
Whether you're advertising an event for a nightclub or organization, club flyers help you generate interest cost-effectively and quickly. Hand them out to passersby in the streets, post them on bulletin boards, or place under vehicle windshield wipers!
- Choose our 14 pt. Gloss Cover for a beautiful shine, the 14 pt. Uncoated Cover for a textured look, or one of our Matte stocks for a more subdued and vintage look. We are also now offering Trifecta triple-layered paper!
- Choose one of our premium paper stocks, such as 13 pt. Premium Linen, 18 pt. Ultra Premium Pearl, or 18 pt. Premium Kraft to create prints that are modern, elegant, or rustic.
- Want to take your prints to the next level? Choose 16 pt. Matte Cover paper stock and take advantage of our Raised UV or Raised Foil specialty finishes.
- High Gloss UV Coating makes the products more durable, so your club flyers will last longer. You cannot write or print on products with High Gloss UV Coating.
- If you'd like to write on the back of your club flyers, choose the 14 pt. Uncoated Cover or select High Gloss UV Coating Front Only on the 14 pt. Gloss Cover.
- Our High Gloss UV Coating Front Only option may produce variation in color and brightness between the front and back of your product.
- Due to the fact that we print in large volumes, different paper types may have different quantity options.
- Aqueous coating will be applied to 250+ quantity orders printed on 100 lb. Gloss Book or 100 lb. Gloss Cover paper stocks.
- Quantities of 100 and fewer printed on 100 lb. Gloss Book or 100 lb. Gloss Cover paper stocks will be printed digitally with no coating.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: What is the purpose of a club flyer?**
Get the word out about your nightclub, organization, or party with stunning, full-color club flyers! Whether you are posting them around town or handing them out to passersby, high-quality, professionally-printed club flyers are a cost-effective way to attract new customers to your business.
**Q: Why should I order my club flyers from GotPrint?**
When you print club flyers through GotPrint, you won't have to skimp on quality to enjoy our affordable prices. Thanks to our state-of-the-art equipment, your club flyers will be printed with rich, vibrant inks that will make your images and text look incredible. Our inexpensive full-color, double-sided printing process allows you to include additional information on the back of your club flyer for a low price! We offer multiple sizes and premium paper stocks for you to choose from.
If you have already designed your club flyer and just need us to print it, no problem. Simply select your flyer preferences, upload your file and place your order. If you don't have an existing design, you can use our helpful Online Designer tool to create one! Our free templates are fully customizable, so you can add/edit text, upload images, and choose from the hundreds of backgrounds and images we offer within the tool. Our club flyers are backed by our 100% satisfaction guarantee for first-time customers, so if you are not happy with your final product, please let us know. If we find that that we have made an error, we will reprint your order as soon as possible.
**Q: How do I make custom club flyers?**
If you need promotional club flyers but don't know where to start, we can help. Our free Online Designer tool has a wide selection of general and industry-specific club flyer templates that you can personalize with your company or event details. Each template is fully editable and can be personalized with your business name, contact information, and any other text you wish to include.
If you would like to design your own club flyer using a graphic design program, we recommend starting with one of our guideline templates. We have templates for all of our club flyer sizes and orientations. These downloadable templates are clearly marked with the safety zone and trim and bleed lines.
If you don't have the time to design your own flyer and would like to hire a graphic designer, our in-house team can help create the perfect print-ready club flyer for your business. Simply add one of our reasonably-priced design packages to your order, provide us with the relevant info and images/logos, and we'll take care of the rest.
