## Notepads
Personalized notepads and memo pads are an essential tool in the office and an effective promotional tool to market your business with a personal touch.
- Notepads can be printed on one or both sides. Choose either 50 or 100 sheets per pad.
- Notepads are glued at the top and backed with a 22 pt. kraft chip board
- We offer 70 lb. Opaque Smooth White paper for all of our stationery, including envelopes and letterheads, for a matching and complete corporate package.
