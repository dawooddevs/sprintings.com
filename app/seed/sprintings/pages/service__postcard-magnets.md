## Postcard Magnets
Whether you are advertising your new business or promoting an upcoming event, our durable postcard magnets are impossible to ignore! GotPrint’s postcard magnets are available in square, landscape or portrait format in a variety of sizes - choose between 4" x 4", 4" x 6" or 5"x 7". A larger postcard magnet gives you the room to display a list of services, menu items or special discounts and promotions in a clean, easy-to-read layout. Postcard magnets are also popular for wedding save the dates, wedding invitations, party invitations, and baby announcements.
Whether you operate a restaurant, home repair or handyman service, retail store, or other small business, invest in high-quality postcard magnets to ensure customers never have to look far for your contact information. Each magnet is printed on a 17 pt. UV-coated durable adhesive material with glossy surface. The adhesive material sticks to all metal-based surfaces, including refrigerators and magnet display boards. Download our templates to design and print your GotPrint postcard magnets today!
