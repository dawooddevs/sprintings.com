## Folded Hang Tags
Print your company logo, contact information, pricing, and any other pertinent information on our folded hang tags. You can design hang tags to attach to gifts for a personal touch.
- Strings are not included with hang tags.
- Choose one of our Glossy stocks for a beautiful shine, the 14 pt. Uncoated Cover for a textured look, or one of our Matte stocks for a more subdued and vintage look.
- High Gloss UV Coating makes the products more durable, so your hang tags will last longer. You cannot write or print on products with High Gloss UV Coating. If you would like to write on the inside of your hang tag, you should choose the 14 pt. Uncoated Cover or select High Gloss UV Coating Front Only on the 14 pt. Gloss Cover.
- For the 14 pt. Gloss paper stock, a High Gloss UV Coating will be applied to the color sides, unless High Gloss UV Coating Front Only is selected. High Gloss UV Coating will not be applied to the back if you select Grayscale Back or Blank Back.
- Our High Gloss UV Coating Front Only option may produce variation in color and brightness between the front and back of your product.
- Due to the fact that we print in large volumes, different paper types may have different quantity options.
- Aqueous coating will be applied to 250+ quantity orders printed on 100 lb. Gloss Book or 100 lb. Gloss Cover paper stocks.
- Quantities of 100 and fewer printed on 100 lb. Gloss Book or 100 lb. Gloss Cover paper stocks will be printed digitally with no coating.

## Custom Folded Hang Tags
Aside from looking sleek and professional, a folded hang tag gives you room while retaining a clean layout. This allows you to include important information such as care instructions, pricing info, or sizes on the inside while allowing your logo/design to dominate the front.
Custom folded hang tags are the perfect solution for customers whom demand a lot of information about their products. If you sell food, you can satisfy them with nutritional facts, while for clothing items, you can add information about the fabric and washing info. Print them on our uncoated card stocks for an easy surface to write on, or order them with a glossy finish to seal in designs and colors.
