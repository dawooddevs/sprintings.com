## Invitations
Your event or gala invitation design is a vital piece of the puzzle. At Sprintings, we understand how a gala invitation design needs to be tied to your organization’s visual identity, but also elevate and highlight the event.

#### We have the perfect invitations to fit your needs:
- Upscale gala
- Business dinner or event
- Retirement party, anniversary party
- Wedding
- Grand opening event

#### Show Some Class with Coordinated Invitation Packages
While shopping for invitations, don’t forget save-the-dates! This precursor to your invitation will set the tone for the event and can be incorporated into your preliminary graphic design efforts.
We recommend having your visual identity in place so we can help you make the most out of each piece of your mailing puzzle: save-the-date, invitation, response cards, and envelopes.
Just like other forms of direct mail, your priority is to get a recipient to open the envelope. If the envelope is not eye-catching, your invitation may get tossed. Let our expert team at Sprintings help you create a beautiful, eye-catching invitation suite.

#### Our Service Promise
Whether it’s sharpness or detail, our best-in-class printers and quality assurance team will make sure that you get superb quality, spectacular invitations. All prints come with a “100% money refund if not satisfied” guarantee.
