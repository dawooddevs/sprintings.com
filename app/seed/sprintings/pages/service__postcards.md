## Postcards
Postcards are a powerful tool in direct marketing. At Sprintings, we have the equipment and knowledge to bring your postcard ideas to reality. Let us design your postcard campaign, and feel secure distributing printed pieces that are sure to grab attention and elicit a strong response.

#### We have the perfect postcards to fit your needs:
- Announce events
- Share promotions and sales
- Remind customers or patients that they’re due for an appointment
- Celebrate customer birthdays and anniversaries

#### Share Clear, Organized Information
Sending a postcard advertisement for your business? Highlight the most important parts of your message with big, bold text or colorful graphics so customers can see and process the information quickly. Include a call to action for a faster response!
Postcards are a powerful marketing tool because they allow you to deliver your message right to customers’ mailboxes. Use them to advertise your services, grand openings, new locations, clearance sales, and more. Drive foot traffic by mailing exclusive coupons or discounts to target neighborhoods.
