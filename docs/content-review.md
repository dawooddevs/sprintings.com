## Product “Folded Business Cards” (/service/folded-business-cards/) — FAQ

> Can you send me a sample of your folded business card?
> Yes - request a free sample kit today! Our sample kit contains a variety of the products we offer, including a folded card sample, so you can see and feel the different paper stocks and coatings for yourself.

> How much do you charge for folded business cards?
> Our 2" x 3.5" (folded size) business cards start at just $40.46 for 100 cards. With our affordable folded business cards, you'll have more room to advertise your products and services, making this a cost-effective investment that you can't pass up.

> Can I upload my own folded business card design?
> Of course - please start by downloading the correct template for the size you wish to order. All of our templates are conveniently marked with the safe zone, trim, and bleed lines. The safe zone is the area where all your critical elements (text, images, logos) should be placed so that they are not cut off during the trimming process. The bleed is the part outside of the safe zone that gets trimmed off when the product is cut to its final size. We require a 0.1" bleed for folded business cards.
> Once your file is ready, simply upload your design and place your order. We have 2 proof options - see an instant online proof (free) or request a manually processed PDF proof to make sure your file has been set up correctly for print.

> I need help with my design. Do you offer any pre-made templates?
> Designing a folded business card is easy with our free Online Designer tool – no graphic designer or expensive software needed! Customize any of our modern, professionally-designed templates with your company name and text. You can even upload your own logo, photos, and other graphics. Once you are happy with your personalized design, place your order and we'll send it straight to print.

> How long will it take to get my folded business cards?
> The production time for most folded cards is 3 to 5 business days. (The day after you have approved your proof and your payment has been processed is considered the first day of production.) If you need a quicker turnaround, rush printing is available for many of our products. Please allow an additional 2 to 6 business days for the cards to arrive at your doorstep; you will have the option to select expedited shipping at checkout if needed.
